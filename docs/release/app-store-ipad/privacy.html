落叶画板隐私政策

本政策适用于从 App Store 下载的落叶画板 iPad 版。

在本机创作

画板无需注册或登录。绘画、素材和音乐可离线使用。开发者不通过 App 收集或接收作品、草稿、导入文件、设备标识或使用记录。App 不含广告、分析跟踪或应用内更新检查。

作品与文件

自动草稿、画夹、绘画录像和偏好设置保存在设备上的 App 数据中。导入时，App 读取你通过系统文件选择器选中的文件。保存或导出时，文件写到你选定的位置。如果你选择 iCloud Drive 或其他文件提供方，该服务按自己的政策处理文件。

移到回收站的作品仍保存在 App 内，可从回收站恢复。若要移除这台设备上的 App 本地数据，请使用系统的「删除 App」。已导出到「文件」等位置的副本请在相应位置删除。

商店购买与联系

下载、付款和更新由 Apple App Store 处理。画板不会读取付款资料。若你主动联系支持，开发者会用你提供的信息处理请求。请勿在公开工单中提交儿童个人信息或私密作品。

Luoye Artboard Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Luoye Artboard for iPad downloaded from the App Store.

Creating on your device

The app does not require an account or sign-in. Drawing tools, artwork assets, and music work offline. The developer does not collect or receive artwork, drafts, imported files, device identifiers, or usage records through the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics tracking, or in-app update checks.

Artwork and files

Drafts, albums, drawing recordings, and preferences are stored in the app's data on your device. When you import a file, the app reads the file you select in the system file picker. When you save or export, the file is written to the location you choose. If you select iCloud Drive or another file provider, that service handles the file under its own policy.

Artwork moved to the trash remains in the app and can be restored. To remove the app’s local data from this device, use Delete App in iPadOS. To delete a copy exported to Files or another location, remove it there.

Purchases and support

Apple's App Store handles downloads, payments, and updates. Luoye Artboard does not read payment details. If you contact support, the developer uses the information you provide to respond to your request. Please keep children's personal information and private artwork out of public issues.
