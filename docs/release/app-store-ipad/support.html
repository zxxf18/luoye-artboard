落叶画板使用支持 · Luoye Artboard Support

备份作品

在画板右上方点「保存」，将工程存到「文件」。以后可用「打开」继续编辑。需要普通图片时，点「导出」并选择 PNG 或 JPEG。

报告问题

请在项目问题页面提供「关于」中显示的版本、iPad 型号、iPadOS 版本和复现步骤。请勿上传儿童个人信息或私密作品。

Backing up your artwork

Tap Save in the toolbar to keep an editable project in Files. Use Open to resume editing later. Tap Export to create a PNG or JPEG copy.

Reporting a problem

Visit the project issue tracker and include the version from About, your iPad model, your iPadOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Keep children's personal information and private artwork out of public posts.
