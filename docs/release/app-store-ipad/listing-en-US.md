# English (U.S.) product page

**Name** Luoye Artboard

**Subtitle** Offline drawing and coloring

**Promotional Text** Pick a brush, add stickers, and bring a picture to life. The art library comes with the app, so you can keep creating offline.

**Description**

Luoye Artboard is an offline drawing studio for iPad. Open a canvas and start drawing, with no account or sign-in required.

- Draw with pencil, watercolor, crayon, and rainbow brushes, or fill areas with the paint bucket.
- Choose backgrounds, stickers, frames, and coloring pages from the library. Stamp patterns and animated stickers as you draw.
- Arrange artwork in layers, preview effects in the Darkroom, and make pixel art or short animations.
- Keep artwork in an on-device album, save an editable project, or export a PNG or JPEG.

A one-time purchase includes the drawing studio and its bundled art library. There are no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

**App Review Notes**

This build is for iPad only. Drawing, bundled assets, music, drafts, and albums work in airplane mode. No demo account or credentials are required. Draw with a finger or Apple Pencil, tap Library to add a bundled asset, or open Darkroom to preview and apply an effect. The privacy policy is available offline from About.
